Why Pragmatic VEX

Stop reading SIGGRAPH papers
like foreign languages.

Remember when dot and cross products stopped being formulas and became tools you could think with? That is the goal here, with the decompositions, tensors and differential operators behind advanced geometry and simulation methods.

01

No academic gatekeeping.

You do not need to prove SVD on a chalkboard to put it to work. See what svddecomp() gives you, how to read its result and how to use it for rigid-transform fitting and shape matching.

02

Demystify the scary buzzwords.

See a Jacobian through local stretching, turning and compression. Read a Hessian through changes in a scalar field's slope. See strain, spin and expansion in a velocity-gradient tensor. Then inspect and use those quantities in Houdini.

03

Advanced methods. Pure VEX.

Weingarten maps. Biharmonic distances. Solenoidal fields through Helmholtz decomposition. Temporal oriented bounding cylinders. Learn the constructions behind the names and make them part of your own technical toolkit.

Created by

Yunus “animatrix” Balcioglu

Senior FX Technical Director at Industrial Light & Magic.

Pragmatic VEX is a highly planned, highly edited series aimed to increase the technical capacity of artists and TDs. This series will enable you to tackle more complex production shots with complete control and ease by acquiring a deeper technical understanding of how things work in Houdini at the lowest level with a strong applied focus on high-end feature film visual effects production.

Through carefully structured lessons, you'll gain the low-level understanding and control required for high-complexity shots in Houdini. The techniques taught in Pragmatic VEX have been proven in major feature film productions, including blockbusters like The LOTR: The Rings of Power, Star Wars, Aquaman, X-Men and more, ensuring you learn production-proven methods used at the highest levels of VFX production.
