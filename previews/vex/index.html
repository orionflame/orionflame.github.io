Inside the volumes

Fundamentals to
advanced applications.

Each volume explores the mathematical and technical foundations needed to design higher-level procedural systems, not just follow UI recipes.

Geometry & topology

Point clouds, geometric distribution, subdivision, limit-surface evaluation, adaptivity.

Fields & tracing

Gradient descent, isolines, streamlines, volume sampling and field transformations.

Linear algebra

PCA, SVD, eigenanalysis, polar decomposition, least squares and shape fitting.

Curvature & shape

Differential geometry, principal curvatures, fundamental forms, Hessians and shape operators.

Vector calculus

Gradient, divergence, curl, Laplacian, Jacobians, deformation and fluid-analysis tensors.

Real-world techniques

ICP, shape matching, field visualization, high-performance VEX and numerical methods.
