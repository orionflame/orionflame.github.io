Inverse ocean displacement

A choppy ocean spectrum moves points horizontally as well as vertically. Projecting collision geometry onto the displaced surface and subtracting a sampled offset does not generally recover the coordinates before deformation. The displacement field and its Jacobian can be used in an iterative solve for the source position whose forward mapping reaches the target. This is an inverse mapping problem, not a reversed vector.

Jacobians · Inverse mapping · Ocean spectra

Principal curvature on smooth surfaces

Surface derivatives describe how the normal and tangent directions change. From the fundamental forms and shape operator, compute principal curvatures and their directions on the evaluated OpenSubdiv limit surface. These quantities can drive surface analysis and directional effects without measuring only the faceted control mesh.

Shape operator · Fundamental forms · OpenSubdiv

Volume 2 · Fitting

Shape matching and point-cloud registration

With corresponding points, covariance and SVD give a least-squares rigid fit. When correspondence is unknown, iterative closest point alternates matching and transform estimation. Both are useful building blocks for alignment, local frames and geometry tools, with initialization and outliers still important to the result.

PCA · SVD · Rigid fitting · ICP

Volume 2 · Field methods

Velocity gradients and field decomposition

The velocity-gradient tensor describes local stretching, compression and spin. Helmholtz decomposition addresses a different question: how a field separates into curl-free and divergence-free contributions, with boundary and harmonic terms handled for the chosen domain. Together, these methods provide ways to inspect and modify specific field behavior.

Velocity gradient · Strain · Helmholtz

Before choosing a course

Common questions

Can I start with Volume 2?

Yes. Volume 1 and Volume 2 are standalone advanced courses. Each develops its own topics from the prerequisites needed inside that course. Choose the volume whose methods are most useful to you, or bundle both.

Is Volume 1 outdated because it began in an earlier Houdini version?

No. Volume 1 has been updated for Houdini 21. The version label describes the current maintained course content, not the age of the first recording.

What knowledge is assumed?

Basic Houdini and VEX familiarity is assumed. The courses do not reteach introductory syntax or interface basics. Supporting documentation is referenced when a prerequisite API or concept is needed.

How is mathematics taught in Pragmatic VEX?

Mathematical ideas are explained through geometric and visual intuition, then developed into working VEX implementations. The emphasis is on why the method works, which assumptions and numerical choices matter, and how to adapt the same reasoning to other geometry, field, deformation and simulation problems.
